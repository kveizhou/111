3. 汇总扫描内容
下面是一整条命令：
python3 -c 'import csv,sys; from collections import Counter; rows=list(csv.DictReader(open(sys.argv[1]),delimiter="\t")); masters={(r["oa_library"],r["oa_cell"]):r for r in rows}; print("PROBE_ROWS="+str(len(rows))); print("PROBE_MASTERS="+str(len(masters))); print("LIBRARIES="+str(sorted({r["oa_library"] for r in rows}))); print("HAS_SCHEMATIC="+str(dict(Counter(r["has_schematic"] for r in masters.values())))); print("PARAMETER_COUNTS="+str(dict(Counter(r["parameter"] for r in rows if r["parameter"] in ("w","l","nfin","fw","fingers","nf","m","r"))))); [print({k:r[k] for k in ("oa_library","oa_cell","model_name","terminal_names","has_schematic")}) for r in list(masters.values())[:20]]' "$st_inputs/pdk_probe.tsv"
预期显示：
PROBE_ROWS=实际记录数
PROBE_MASTERS=实际master数
LIBRARIES=实际库名列表
HAS_SCHEMATIC=...
PARAMETER_COUNTS=...
